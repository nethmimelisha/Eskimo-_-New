OUR STORY

Sri Lankan Knitwear Manufacturer


A Knitwear Manufacturer in Sri Lanka

Eskimo Fashion Knitwear (Private) Limited manufactures knitwear in Sri Lanka. The company’s history dates to 1870, and its Sri Lankan business was incorporated in 1982.
