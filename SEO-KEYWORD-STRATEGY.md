# International SEO keyword strategy

## Objective

Attract qualified international apparel brands and sourcing teams researching knitwear manufacturers in Sri Lanka. Keep one English-language site for buyers in Australia, Germany, France, Italy, the UK and other European markets. Do not imply that Eskimo currently exports to or serves those markets.

## Search-intent map

| Priority | Query cluster | Primary page | Implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core commercial | knitwear manufacturer; knitwear manufacturer Sri Lanka; Sri Lankan knitwear manufacturer | Home | Make the company, manufacturing location and knitwear focus explicit in the title, H1 and opening copy. |
| Manufacturing evaluation | knitwear manufacturing process Sri Lanka; knitwear production facilities Sri Lanka | Process & Technology; Plants | Explain the documented processes and facilities, then link to products and B2B contact. |
| Product discovery | knitted accessories manufacturer; knitted apparel products Sri Lanka | Products | Describe only the product categories shown in the gallery and link to process, facilities and contact. |
| Company evaluation | knitwear manufacturing company Sri Lanka; Eskimo knitwear | About; Brands; Certifications | Provide company history and published materials without implying current customer relationships or certification status beyond the displayed evidence. |
| Inquiry | contact knitwear manufacturer Sri Lanka; knitwear sourcing enquiry | Contact | Make a direct B2B enquiry the next step from core manufacturing pages. |

## Terms requiring commercial verification

The phrases **private label knitwear manufacturer** and **sustainable knitwear manufacturer** describe specific capabilities or positioning. They are intentionally not asserted in site copy or structured data until Eskimo confirms the service scope and provides evidence supporting the sustainability claim. If verified, build those topics into existing process or sustainability content before considering a dedicated page.

Likewise, confirm that brand logos and every displayed certification or affiliation may be published and accurately describe current relationships/status. The certifications page now directs buyers to verify current validity and scope.

## International targeting

- Keep all pages in English and use the same canonical URLs for every market.
- Do not create near-duplicate country pages, automatic geo-redirects or market-specific claims.
- Use Search Console country and query reports to compare impressions and qualified enquiries from Australia, Germany, France, Italy, the UK and other European countries.
- Keep terminology understandable to international procurement teams; do not add translated terms to an English page without a localization decision.

## Measurement and next research

This is an intent-led map based on the buyer queries supplied by the client, not a claim about search volume, ranking difficulty or current rankings. Validate demand and refine priorities using Google Keyword Planner and Search Console after the site is live and collecting data. Track organic enquiries alongside impressions, clicks, click-through rate and landing-page performance.
